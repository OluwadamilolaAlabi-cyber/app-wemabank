    Invest in the Dangote Refinery IPO on ALAT

    The Dangote Petroleum Refinery IPO gives investors an opportunity to subscribe for shares in Dangote Petroleum Refinery & Petrochemicals FZE.

    Wema Bank is a Receiving Bank for the offer, making it possible for customers to submit their applications and subscription funds through the ALAT by Wema app.

    There will be 4.1 billion ordinary shares on offer when the IPO opens on Monday, September 14, 2026. The offer is open for subscription subject to the terms and conditions in the Prospectus.

    Get started →
    Offer Opens: 14 Sept 2026 Offer Closes: 13 Oct 2026 Shares on Offer: 4.1 billion ordinary shares Offer Price: ₦525 per share Min Subscription: 10 shares (₦5,250) Offer Opens: 14 Sept 2026 Offer Closes: 13 Oct 2026 Shares on Offer: 4.1 billion ordinary shares Offer Price: ₦525 per share Min Subscription: 10 shares (₦5,250)

    How to Subscribe for Dangote Refinery Shares on ALAT

    1. Update your ALAT appOpen your app store/Google Play store and update ALAT.
    2. Open ALATSign in to your ALAT app.
    3. Go to WealthTap Menu at the bottom of your screen, then select Wealth.
    4. Select IPOTap IPO under the investment options.
    5. Select Dangote IPOChoose Dangote IPO under the available offers.
    6. Follow the instructionsReview the offer details and complete your application and payment.
    Investing for the First Time?

    You do not need a CSCS account or CHN to start your application. Your CSCS and CHN details will be sent to you by email or SMS on or before two business days, as applicable, after your application and subscription funds have been submitted.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. What is an IPO?

    An Initial Public Offering (IPO) is an offer through which a company makes shares available to investors for subscription.

    2. What is the Dangote Refinery IPO?

    It is an offer for subscription for 4.1 billion ordinary shares of Dangote Petroleum Refinery & Petrochemicals FZE.

    3. What is the price of the shares?

    The offer price is ₦525 per share.

    4. What is the minimum number of shares I can subscribe for?

    The minimum subscription is 10 shares, costing ₦5,250.

    5. When does the offer open?

    The offer opens on 14 September 2026.

    6. When does the offer close?

    The offer closes on 13 October 2026.

    7. How can I subscribe through Wema Bank?

    You can subscribe through ALAT.

    8. How do I subscribe on ALAT?

    Open ALAT, tap Menu, select Wealth, tap IPO, select Dangote IPO and follow the instructions.

    9. Do I need a CSCS account to apply?

    No. You do not need a CSCS account or CHN to start your application.

    10. How will I get my CSCS and CHN details?

    Your CSCS and CHN details will be sent to you by email or SMS on or before two business days, as applicable, after your application and subscription funds have been submitted.

    11. Can I apply if I am new to investing?

    Yes. You can apply as a first-time investor, subject to the eligibility and requirements stated in the offer documents.

    12. Do I need an ALAT account to subscribe through ALAT?

    Yes. You need an active ALAT account to subscribe through the app.

    13. What if I do not have an ALAT account?

    You can download ALAT on the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android) and open an account in less than 5 minutes before subscribing.

    14. Is the investment Shariah-compliant?

    The shares have been determined to be Shariah-compliant based on an independent Shariah assessment and applicable screening criteria.

    15. Where can I find the full details of the offer?

    Please read the Prospectus and other official offer documents carefully before subscribing.

    16. Is subscribing for the shares risk-free?

    No. Investing in shares involves risks. You should understand the offer and consider your financial circumstances before investing.

    17. Can I change or cancel my application after submitting it?

    This is subject to the terms and conditions of the offer. Please review the Prospectus for details.

    18. Who can I speak to if I need professional investment advice?

    You may consult a stockbroker, fund or portfolio manager, accountant, banker, solicitor or other professional adviser registered by the SEC. Alternatively, you can contact the Wema Bank Customer Care.

    19. Where can I subscribe on ALAT?

    Go to Menu → Wealth → IPO → Dangote IPO and follow the instructions.

    20. How do I get started?

    Update your ALAT app, make sure you have sufficient funds, read the offer documents and subscribe when the offer opens on 14 September 2026.
